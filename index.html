Frame privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Who we are

Frame runs the Frame photo-sharing app. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: frameofficialapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

Account: your email address and password (or your Google account details if you sign in with Google).

Profile: display name, username, bio and profile picture.

Content: photos, stories, captions and comments you post. When you add tags to a photo, they are private to you. Other people never see them, but they help your photo show up when someone searches for that tag.

Activity: likes, follows, saved photos, reports, notifications, and a record that an account viewed a photo (used to count views).

Payments: if you set up payouts, the PayPal email you give us. If you buy an ad campaign, PayPal confirms the payment to us. We do not receive or store your card number.

Advertising and device data: Google AdMob may use your device advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. Our hosting provider keeps technical logs such as IP address and device or app version.

How we use it

To run your account and show your content, to keep Frame safe (for example handling reports and spotting abuse), to count views and calculate creator earnings, to process ad payments and payouts, to show ads, and to fix problems and improve the app.

Who can see your content

Public photos, your profile and your username can be seen by other Frame users. Photos you mark private are visible only to you. Stories disappear after 24 hours. Your email address, payout details and tags are not shown to other users.

Who we share data with

We use service providers to run Frame: Supabase (hosting, sign-in, database and photo storage), Google (AdMob advertising and optional Google sign-in) and PayPal (ad payments and creator payouts). They process data for us under their own terms. We may also disclose data if the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

Ads and your choices

Frame shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first. You can review or change your advertising choices from the settings in your profile when that option is shown, and you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone settings.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. Stories are removed after 24 hours. When you delete a photo it is removed from Frame. To delete your account and the data tied to it, email frameofficialapp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We may keep limited records where the law requires it, for example payment records, or where needed to prevent abuse.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict some uses, and to withdraw consent. Email your-email@example.com to use these rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States and Singapore, which may have different data protection laws from yours. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security

We use encryption in transit and access controls so people can only read the data they are allowed to see. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Frame is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email your-email@example.com and we will delete it. In some countries the minimum age is higher, and you must meet your local minimum age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make an important change we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
